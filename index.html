      Help

      • Search: Just start typing on the home screen, then press Enter.
      • Menu (LSK): Opens Favorites, History, Settings and Help. Press 1-4 to jump to an item.
      • Back (RSK): Returns to the previous screen, or closes StartPage from Home.
      • Favorites: Pick "Add favorite" to save a site. Press Enter to open one, or Options to open/delete.
      • History: Recent searches and sites, newest first. Enter re-opens one; Options deletes it or clears everything.
      • Settings: Change the default search engine and toggle Day/Dark theme.
